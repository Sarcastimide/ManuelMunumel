About Me

Hello —

I'm Manuel.

I'm a tattoo artist and pointillist. My work is built one dot at a time — thousands of tiny points of ink that, together, become light, shadow and form. It's slow, meticulous work, and that patience is exactly what draws me to it.

I work in two worlds that feed each other: pointillism on paper and tattooing on skin. The same language of stippling and dotwork runs through both — fine, deliberate marks that build depth without a single solid line.

Most of what I do is by appointment. If you're after a custom dotwork tattoo or an original pointillist piece, get in touch — I take on a limited number of projects so each one gets the attention it deserves.
